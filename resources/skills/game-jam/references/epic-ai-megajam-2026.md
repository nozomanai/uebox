# 2026 Epic AI MegaJam

Source: https://itch.io/jam/2026-epic-ai-megajam and the Official Rules linked from it
(English PDF, also available in Chinese). Check the page for changes before relying on a date.

## Dates

| Event                   | Pacific (official)      | Beijing            |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------ |
| Theme reveal livestream | Nov 11, 11:00 AM PST    | Nov 12, 03:00      |
| Jam starts              | Nov 12, 12:00 AM EST    | Nov 12, 13:00      |
| Daily livestreams       | 11:00 AM PST            | 03:00 next day     |
| **Submission deadline** | **Nov 15, 5:59 PM PST** | **Nov 16, 09:59**  |
| Winners announced       | by Jan 29, 2027         |                    |

Late submissions are disqualified. Upload well before the deadline.

## Who and how

- Age 18+, active Epic Games account (not a cabined account). Solo or up to five people,
  any number of AI agents. One submission per team. Unreal Engine **or** UEFN, not both.
- Restricted jurisdictions are listed in the Official Rules; mainland China is not among them.

## Unreal Engine submission (on itch.io)

1. Windows **Development** build, packaged. Unpackaged projects are rejected. The game must
   run without extra software.
2. Zip named `TeamName_GameName`, under 1 GB (larger: butler or Blendo Itch Uploader).
3. Upload to your own itch.io account, then submit to the jam with:
   - team name, every member's name and the email of their Epic account
   - game name
   - gameplay video link, 30–60 seconds
   - dev log link (optional; required for the Glass Box prize)
4. **Everything in English**: in-game text, audio, the video and the entry information.
5. List sourced (non-original) assets. AI-generated content is allowed and is not a sourced
   asset.
6. Gameplay must go beyond Epic's starter templates and use the theme.

## Judging (1–5 each)

Ambition and innovation · Theme · Fun factor · Gameplay · Visuals · Level design and
progression · Audio.

## Prizes

Main: Best Overall UE, Best Overall UEFN, Best Student Project — $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500.
Modifiers: Best Team $5,000; Solo Driver, Render Royalty (visuals), Pocket Powerhouse (mobile),
Glass Box (dev log), Walkie-Talkie (UEFN only) $2,500 each. Development Continuation Grants
up to $50,000.

## Prepared before the jam

Allowed: AI tools, agent configuration, MCP settings, prompt libraries, scripts. Epic may ask
for them or for version-control history. Not allowed: gameplay, levels or art made before
Nov 12 — declare anything prepared earlier.
